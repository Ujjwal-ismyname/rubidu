Terms, refunds and who makes Rubidu

Rubidu is made and sold by one independent developer. This page says in plain words what you are buying and what happens if it is not for you. The full licence agreement is shown in the app when you install it.

Who I am

Ujjwal Aggarwal, an independent developer in India. Write to heyrubidu@gmail.com for anything: questions, a refund, a problem, or a privacy request.

Refunds

If Rubidu is not for you, ask for your money back within 14 days of buying it, by email or through the shop you bought it from, and you will get it. This does not take away any right the law gives you where you live.

What you are buying

  • A licence to install and use Rubidu on Apple-silicon Macs that you own or use, and to keep a backup copy. You cannot resell, rent, copy or take apart the app.
  • The app works offline once it is set up. It sends nothing about you or your use to me or to anyone.
  • Updates are never installed on their own. You choose whether to install one.
  • Rubidu is software with an AI model that sometimes gets things wrong. It is not for emergencies or for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions, and it is provided as it is, without warranty, within what the law allows.

Payment and taxes

Payment, receipts and any sales tax or VAT are handled by the shop that sells Rubidu, which is the seller you pay. Their terms about payment and delivery apply to the purchase.

Privacy

Rubidu has no account and no tracking. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. See [the privacy page](privacy.html) and [how to check that Rubidu is private](is-rubidu-private.html).

Questions

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within 14 days of buying and you get your money back.

Can I use Rubidu on more than one Mac?

On Apple-silicon Macs that you own or use, yes.
