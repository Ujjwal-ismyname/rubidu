Privacy notice

Last updated 9 October 2026

Who runs this site

Ujjwal, a student in India building Rubidu. I decide what happens to the data below, which makes me the “data fiduciary” under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act. To reach me about your data, use the feedback form (pick “Something else”) and include how to reach you.

Just visiting

There are no cookies, no ads and no third-party trackers, and the fonts and code are served from this site itself. To know whether anyone is reading, the page counts visits, in the simplest way I could build:

  • What is counted: that a page was viewed, which page, the site that sent you (for example “instagram.com”, or “direct”), the kind of device (Mac, iPhone, Android, Windows…), and whether it is your browser’s first visit that day.
  • What is not: no name, no ID, no cookie and no IP address. Nothing that could tell me who you are, and nothing that follows you to other sites.
  • How: your browser remembers only today’s date (in its local storage, on your device), so a second visit the same day isn’t counted as a new visitor. The counts go to the same private Google Sheet as the forms below.
  • Not counted at all if your browser sends “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you join the waitlist

  • What I receive: your email, whether you want test builds, the site that sent you, and any answers you choose to give afterwards (your kind of Mac, what you’d use, what you’d pay).
  • Why: to email you when Rubidu is ready, to send test builds if you asked for them, and to count how many people want it and what for. Nothing else: no newsletters you didn’t ask for, and never sold or shared.
  • Where it is kept: the same private Google Sheet as the feedback, which only I can open.
  • How long: until Rubidu launches and I’ve emailed you about it, then at most 12 months more. Reply to any email I send, or use the feedback form, and I’ll remove you straight away.

If you send the feedback form

  • What I receive: the topic you pick, your message, “You are” (optional), your email (optional), and the page address.
  • Why: to read your feedback and make Rubidu better. Your email is used only to reply to you.
  • Where it is kept: a private Google Sheet in my Google account, which only I can open. Google stores it as my service provider, possibly outside India.
  • Who else sees it: no one. I never sell it or use it for marketing, and I don’t use it to train AI models. I may use AI tools to help me sort feedback, with email addresses removed first.
  • How long: until 12 months after Rubidu launches, then deleted.

Under 18?

You’re welcome to send feedback, but please leave your email and other personal details out. If you think someone under 18 has sent me personal data, tell me and I’ll delete it.

Your rights

You can ask what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. I’ll reply within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

The Rubidu app

This notice is about the website. Rubidu itself runs on your Mac: your voice, screen and files are not sent anywhere. The app ships with its own privacy notice.
